## CombatPhysics finding

`QueryRay` uses fixed-point narrowphase. `CombatQueryResult` carries stable entity/body/collider/shape identity plus distance/point/normal and a deterministic comparer (`CombatQueryResult.cs:68-132,199-232`). It does not carry material or explicit static classification. Material exists on colliders and the contact runner demonstrates shape+body/collider lookup; static selection is currently a layer-mask convention.

T2 stop gate: pure Combat tests must prove static-mask exclusion, exact primitive/compound-leaf identity and material lookup before ground-probe implementation. Failure requires a general Combat fix; Unity Raycast/Terrain/Collider is forbidden.
