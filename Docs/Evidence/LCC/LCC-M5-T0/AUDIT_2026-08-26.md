## 3. Previous/current animation witness and schema decision

The witness is a fixed-capacity same-tick scratch copy of the selected leaf identity plus its complete fixed-point shape pose and owner root position:

- previous: copied from the prior completed/restore-rebuilt DamageGeometry snapshot before frame-N motor mutation;
- current: copied from the final frame-N snapshot after SyncAll #2;
- validity: consecutive frame, same entity/source binding revision+hash/logical collider/shape kind/PartId, valid active leaf, legal root delta, no restore suppression/teleport/source mismatch, and compatible topology;
- failure: surface-motion contribution fails closed. Current penetration may still produce bounded separation pressure only when the current leaf is fully valid; presentation phases establish a silent baseline after spawn/restore/source reset.

No schema 34 is required for the frozen implementation. The two shape witnesses are derived within one tick from existing formal animation/action/root/authority-pose/topology state; restore already rebuilds the prior snapshot. Resulting gameplay contact velocity/count/displacement already participate in CharacterTest snapshot/hash/rollback (`CharacterTestNetworkSnapshot.cs:1077-1081,1812-1816,2185-2189,2396-2404`). Started/Stayed/Ended are presentation-only and reset on restore; they cannot affect pressure or future commands.

Schema-34 stop gate: stop T2 before production mutation if it needs a witness cache surviving a tick/restore, if a contact phase affects gameplay, or if rollback+1 cannot reconstruct the previous snapshot. Do not add a private cross-frame cache as a workaround.

## 5. M1 pressure reuse seam

M5 reuses, without copying Combat separation math:

- the three pressure iterations (`ContactPressureIterations = 3`);
- effective mass, effective PushScale and target `CanBePushed` transfer;
- fixed-point `ApplyBodyPresenceSurfacePressure` / `ApplyContactPressure` behavior;
- profile-derived composed-speed clamp and penetration-bias bounds;
- the existing `CharacterTestCharacterPhysicsComponent` contact velocity/count state, consumed by the next motor tick through static sweep.

M5 needs a feature-specific constraint buffer and builder but no direct `SetTransform`, no `ApplyContactDisplacement`, and no change to the two-pass root overlap solver. It may append velocity/count observations to the same resolver accumulators and call `ApplyCharacterContactState` again after SyncAll #2 so M1 and M5 contributions form one frame-N contact state. Source owner and target must both be alive, enabled and `BlocksCharacters`; target movement still respects `CanBePushed`. Pressure policy is the authored source `pressureScaleMilli`; `skinMillimeters` only expands the soft-contact threshold and cannot enter the hard obstacle mask.

Source anchors: `CharacterTestCharacterContactResolver.cs:146-155,480-572,1220-1335,1425-1462,1513-1675`; next-tick motor consumption: `CharacterTestCharacterMotionSystem.cs:1184-1203`.

## 6. Severed timing and contact phases

Combat trace runs after final Hurtbox SyncAll and the M5 seam. Therefore a tail sever accepted during frame N combat becomes geometry topology at frame N+1 SyncAll #1/#2; it is not retroactively removed from the already-consumed frame-N snapshot. At the N+1 final snapshot, `TryResolveActiveLeafCount` and the compound update omit the Severed tail leaves while retaining collider ordinals (`CharacterTestCharacterHurtboxSystem.cs:310-369,410-420,496-519`).

Frozen phase rule: previous active/current missing at that topology boundary yields exactly one presentation `Ended`; current active/previous missing yields `Started`; both active with same canonical key yields `Stayed`. Phase key is `(sourceEntityId, targetEntityId, logicalColliderId, combatColliderId, shapeKind)`. Sort canonically before phase diff. Restore/source reset establishes a silent baseline, so replay/late join does not manufacture Started/Ended.
